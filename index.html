Frequently Asked Questions

What technologies do you use?

I specialize in React.js, Node.js, PHP/Laravel for full-stack development, with MySQL and MongoDB for databases. For frontend, I use HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), and Bootstrap. I also handle DevOps with cPanel, VPS, Vercel, Netlify, and Linux servers.

Do you provide hosting services?

Yes! I offer complete hosting management including domain setup, SSL certificates, DNS configuration, VPS management, cPanel administration, and deployments on cloud platforms like Vercel and Netlify.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple website takes 1-2 weeks, a mid-level web application 3-6 weeks, and complex systems with admin dashboards can take 2-3 months. I always provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase.

What industries have you worked in?

I've delivered projects across e-commerce, healthcare, education, tourism, automotive, restaurants, legal services, logistics, and digital marketing — serving clients in the UK, Switzerland, France, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Iraq.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Absolutely. I provide ongoing maintenance, updates, and support after project delivery. This includes bug fixes, performance optimization, security updates, and feature enhancements as needed.

Do all projects include admin dashboards?

Yes, all my web application projects come with fully-integrated admin dashboards and professional operational systems, giving you complete control over your content, users, and data.
