'''Khaled Ahmed Haggagy''' ({{lang-ar|خالد أحمد حجاجي}}; born 1 March 2001), known professionally as '''Khaled Ahmed''', is an [[Egypt]]ian [[full stack developer|full stack web developer]], software entrepreneur, and coding instructor. He is the founder and lead developer of '''Barmagly''', a software services startup based in [[Egypt]] operating under a Swiss technology partnership.<ref name="barmagly">{{Cite web|url=https://barmagly.tech |title=Barmagly — Software Services |access-date=28 April 2026 |work=Barmagly}}</ref> Ahmed has delivered more than 30 production-grade web projects for clients in seven countries — the [[United Kingdom]], [[Switzerland]], [[France]], the [[United Arab Emirates]], the [[Saudi Arabia|Kingdom of Saudi Arabia]], [[Egypt]], and [[Iraq]] — covering the [[e-commerce]], [[health care|healthcare]], [[education]], [[tourism]], [[automotive industry|automotive]], [[restaurant]], [[law firm|legal]], [[logistics]], and [[digital marketing]] sectors.<ref name="portfolio">{{Cite web|url=https://khaledahmed.net |title=Khaled Ahmed — Personal Website & Portfolio |access-date=28 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="linkedin">{{Cite web|url=https://linkedin.com/in/khaled-ahmed-82368819b |title=Khaled Ahmed — LinkedIn Profile |access-date=28 April 2026 |work=LinkedIn}}</ref>

He holds a [[Bachelor's degree|Bachelor of Science]] in Information Technology from [[Luxor University]] and a professional diploma in Full Stack Web Development ([[PHP]]/[[Laravel]]) from the [[Information Technology Institute (Egypt)|Information Technology Institute (ITI)]]. In addition to his industry work, Ahmed has held several teaching roles in [[Egypt]], the [[Saudi Arabia|Kingdom of Saudi Arabia]], the [[United Arab Emirates]], [[Switzerland]], and [[Sweden]], training students in coding, web development, and computer science.<ref name="linkedin"/>

==Early life and education==

===Background===
Khaled Ahmed was born on '''1 March 2001''' in the town of [[Qift]] in [[Qena Governorate]], in the [[Upper Egypt]] region of southern [[Egypt]]. He was raised in Qift and later relocated to [[Cairo]] for professional work after completing his higher education.

===Higher education===
Between September 2019 and June 2023, Ahmed studied at the [[Luxor University|Faculty of Computers and Information of Luxor University]], where he earned a [[Bachelor's degree|Bachelor of Science]] in '''Information Technology'''. His undergraduate coursework covered programming fundamentals, web technologies, databases, software engineering, and the [[Internet of things]].<ref name="luxor">{{Cite web|url=https://luxor.edu.eg/ |title=Luxor University — Faculty of Computers and Information |access-date=28 April 2026 |work=Luxor University}}</ref>

In October 2023, Ahmed enrolled in an intensive four-month diploma program in '''Full Stack Web Development ([[PHP]]/[[Laravel]])''' at the [[Information Technology Institute (Egypt)|Information Technology Institute (ITI)]] in its [[Qena]] branch, which he completed in February 2024. The ITI program covered server-side development, [[REST]]ful API design, [[Model–view–controller|MVC]] architecture, deployment, and team-based project delivery.<ref name="iti">{{Cite web|url=https://iti.gov.eg/ |title=Information Technology Institute (ITI) |access-date=28 April 2026 |work=Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (Egypt)}}</ref>

===Graduation project===
Ahmed's undergraduate graduation project, the '''Smart Wheelchair IoT System''', was an [[Internet of things|IoT]]-powered wheelchair designed to support users with mobility challenges. The system combined an [[Arduino]] microcontroller, [[Bluetooth]] connectivity, and a [[Flutter (software)|Flutter]] mobile application, and supported two control modes: voice commands and on-screen button controls. The project source code is published on [[GitHub]].<ref name="grad">{{Cite web|url=https://github.com/khaled312001/Smart-Wheelchair-Graduation-Project |title=Smart Wheelchair Graduation Project |access-date=28 April 2026 |work=GitHub}}</ref>

'''Technologies used:''' [[Flutter (software)|Flutter]], [[Arduino]] [[C (programming language)|C]]/[[C++]], [[HTML]]/[[CSS]], [[Internet of things|IoT]] hardware integration.

==Career==
Ahmed began his professional software career in May 2021 while still an undergraduate. Over the following years he built a portfolio of full-stack engineering, instruction, and entrepreneurial roles, working remotely and on-site with companies based in [[Egypt]], [[Switzerland]], [[Sweden]], the [[United Arab Emirates]], and the [[Saudi Arabia|Kingdom of Saudi Arabia]].<ref name="linkedin"/>

===Web Developer at Xappee (2025–present)===
In December 2025, Ahmed joined '''[[Xappee]]''', a technology company headquartered in the [[United Kingdom]] with operations in [[Cairo]], as a full-time on-site Web Developer. In this role he is responsible for:

* Developing production-ready websites by converting [[Figma]] and [[Adobe XD]] designs into pixel-perfect, fully responsive pages.
* Building backend services and [[REST]]ful APIs using [[Laravel]] and [[Node.js]] for data-driven applications.
* Managing web hosting, [[Domain Name System|domain]] configuration, [[Transport Layer Security|SSL]] certificates, server deployment, and performance optimization.
* Delivering projects for clients in the United Kingdom and across Europe, including '''[[#Notable projects|Xappee]]''', '''Ant Assist''', and '''Grand Botanical Suite'''.<ref name="xappee">{{Cite web|url=https://xappee.com |title=Xappee |access-date=28 April 2026}}</ref>

===Coding & Computer Instructor at Green Arrow Academy (2025)===
From May 2025 to October 2025, Ahmed served as a full-time on-site Coding & Computer Instructor at '''Green Arrow Academy''' in the [[Saudi Arabia|Kingdom of Saudi Arabia]]. His responsibilities included:

* Teaching coding fundamentals and computer science to middle school and high school students.
* Designing structured lesson plans covering programming logic, web development, and [[computational thinking]].
* Mentoring students on coding projects to foster [[problem solving|problem-solving]] skills.<ref name="green">{{Cite web|url=https://greenarrow.itegypt.org |title=Green Arrow Academy |access-date=28 April 2026}}</ref>

===ICT Teacher at Nile International Schools (2024–2025)===
Between July 2024 and May 2025, Ahmed worked full-time on-site in [[Egypt]] as an [[Information and communications technology|ICT]] Teacher at '''Nile International Schools'''. His duties included:

* Delivering a comprehensive ICT curriculum aligned with international education standards.
* Integrating modern digital tools and interactive technologies into classroom instruction.
* Assessing student progress through practical assignments and project-based evaluations.<ref name="linkedin"/>

===Coding Instructor at ETRC Academy (2022–present)===
Since March 2022, Ahmed has worked part-time as a remote Coding Instructor at '''ETRC Academy''' in [[Egypt]]. His role involves:

* Conducting live remote coding sessions for beginner and intermediate-level learners.
* Creating curriculum materials covering [[HTML]], [[CSS]], [[JavaScript]], [[PHP]], and [[Python (programming language)|Python]].
* Providing one-on-one mentorship and code reviews to accelerate student growth.<ref name="linkedin"/>

===Web Developer at Neo Soft Hub (2022–2024)===
From February 2022 to February 2024, Ahmed worked under contract as a remote Web Developer for '''Neo Soft Hub''', a Swiss software company. During this two-year tenure he:

* Built and maintained responsive websites using [[HTML]], [[CSS]], [[JavaScript]], [[Bootstrap (front-end framework)|Bootstrap]], and [[Laravel]].
* Implemented frontend features and integrated backend APIs to deliver full-featured web solutions.
* Managed hosting and deployment, including [[CPanel|cPanel]] administration, [[Virtual private server|VPS]] configuration, and domain management.<ref name="linkedin"/>

===Full Stack Developer at Albahith Academy (2022–2023)===
Between June 2022 and August 2023, Ahmed served under contract as a remote Full Stack Developer for '''Albahith Academy''' in the [[United Arab Emirates]]. His responsibilities included:

* Developing end-to-end web applications spanning frontend, backend, and database design.
* Utilizing [[React (software)|React.js]], [[Node.js]], and [[MongoDB]] to build dynamic, data-driven applications.
* Collaborating with remote teams across multiple time zones to deliver projects on schedule.<ref name="linkedin"/>

===Full Stack Development Instructor at Xcodify (2021–2024)===
From May 2021 to October 2024, Ahmed worked part-time as a remote Full Stack Development Instructor for '''Xcodify''' in [[Sweden]]. Over more than three years he:

* Taught full-stack web development courses covering frontend, backend, and deployment.
* Guided students through projects using [[React (software)|React]], [[Node.js]], [[Laravel]], and relational/non-relational databases.
* Developed course content that achieved high student satisfaction and completion rates.<ref name="linkedin"/>

===Founder & Lead Developer of Barmagly (2021–present)===
In May 2021, Ahmed founded '''Barmagly''', an [[Egypt]]-based software services startup operating under a Swiss technology partnership. As Founder and Lead Developer he:

* Manages the startup, overseeing project delivery from concept to deployment.
* Leads full-stack development using [[Laravel]], [[Node.js]], and [[React (software)|React]] for client projects.
* Handles hosting, server administration, client relations, and overall technical architecture.<ref name="barmagly"/>

The Barmagly portfolio includes more than 30 production projects, all of which integrate fully featured admin dashboards and professional operational systems.<ref name="portfolio"/>
